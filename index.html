Ghost Loop – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Ghost Loop is a puzzle game. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

What the app stores

Your level progress (which levels you solved and with how many ghosts) and your settings (language, vibration, sound, music) are saved only on your device. They never leave it. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No analytics, tracking or crash reporting services
  • No internet access: the app does not request the internet permission
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone

Children

Because no personal data is collected, no data from children is collected either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.

Uygulamanın yapmadıkları
